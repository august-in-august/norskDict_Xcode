Slå opp

An offline learner’s dictionary built on Bokmålsordboka and Nynorskordboka. 186 912 articles, with English and Chinese translations.

Native first
SF Pro and PingFang, iOS system greys, grouped lists, segmented controls and switches. The one custom colour is the accent, taken from the red in the brand system.
Norwegian leads
Definitions at 17pt ink; translations at 15pt secondary grey with a small EN / 中 marker. Sense numbers in the accent, in their own column, so long entries scan.
BM / NN
A warm red tag for Bokmål and a blue tag for Nynorsk, used the same way in results, entries, bookmarks and history.
01

iPhone · Light

White theme. Every frame is live: type, tap results, toggle EN/中文, collapse sections, swipe between Bokmål and Nynorsk.

1 · Search, empty
Search field on top, recent lookups below with the base word each form resolved to.
2 · Search as you type
“bøker”. Grouped by dictionary, with a match note; near spellings under Similar headwords.
2b · Similar headwords
“bøkker” matches the Bokmål word and suggests bøkkar (NN).
3 · Results, Bokmål tab
After Return. Segmented control shows counts (Nynorsk has 1); swipe switches tabs; the last tab is remembered.
4a · Entry, translations off
Norwegian only. Fixed expressions and Inflection collapsed; the inflection header previews the forms.
4b · Entry, EN + 中文 on
Translations sit under each definition and example in a quieter style. Machine-translated label with Report error.
4c · Entry, sections expanded
Same entry scrolled down: fixed expressions and the inflection table, searched form highlighted.
4d · Entry, Nynorsk tab
ven, venn — both lemma variants in one table. Reached from “vennen” so that form is highlighted.
4e · Verb entry from “gikk”
Sub-senses (1.1) and a verb inflection row: gå · går · gikk · har gått.
5 · Bookmarks
Short EN and 中文 gloss with BM/NN badge. Swipe left to remove (first row shown mid-swipe).
6 · History
Grouped by day. Clear asks for confirmation.
7 · Settings
Theme swatches (each half shows the light and dark variant), appearance, default translations, language, text size.
7b · Sources
Licence credits and the machine-translation note.
